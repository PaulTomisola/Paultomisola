Experience

I have spent more time than I'd like to admit talking to people. Some behind a microphone. Some through a headset. Some across a desk. Some through a screen. Some on a page. I've hosted radio shows, interviewed artists, talked football, talked music, coordinated dispatch operations across Canada and the US, and written for the web. Different people. Different rooms. Different reasons for the conversation. What has stayed with me, though, is the work itself: listening before speaking, asking better questions, finding clarity when people can't see it for themselves, understanding why people think the way they do, and trying to leave every conversation a little better than I found it.
